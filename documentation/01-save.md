# Save profiles and posts

Keep the accounts and posts you want to come back to, while you browse
Instagram. Profiles and posts are kept apart: saving a post doesn't save its
account, and the other way round.

## Save profiles

### Where it works

```
Save profiles
│
├── Profile page
│   ├── Posts tab
│   ├── Reels tab
│   ├── Tagged tab
│   └── Saved tab (your own profile)
├── Posts
│   ├── Home feed
│   ├── Post page (opened from a link)
│   └── Post window
│       ├── opened from a profile's grid
│       └── opened from Explore
├── Reels
│   ├── the Reels feed
│   └── opened from a reel link
├── Stories
│   ├── opened from the stories row on the home page
│   ├── opened from a profile picture
│   └── opened from a link
├── Highlights
│   ├── opened from a profile page
│   └── opened from a link
├── Dragging a profile link onto any Instagram page
├── KeepKeep's toolbar icon
│   ├── the owner of the page you are on (profile, post, reel, story)
│   └── the owner of each post in your saved Media
└── Import from a backup
```

On posts, reels, stories and highlights it saves the account that posted
them.

### What happens

- The profile is saved at once. On Instagram, a small card then offers to put
  it in one of your lists.
- Saved profiles are marked as saved wherever they appear. Click again to
  remove it; a moment later you can still undo.

### What KeepKeep keeps

- The username and the name
- A small copy of the profile picture (Instagram's own picture links expire)
- When you saved it
- Your lists it is in

### Where you find it

- **KeepKeep's toolbar icon** → Profiles
- **The KeepKeep app** → Profiles. Click a profile to see it in a side panel,
  with the posts you saved from it.

## Save posts

Posts, reels, videos, albums, stories and highlight stories: everything that
isn't a profile is kept in **Media**.

### Where it works

```
Save posts
│
├── Posts
│   ├── Home feed
│   ├── Post page (opened from a link)
│   └── Post window
│       ├── opened from a profile's grid
│       └── opened from Explore
├── Small pictures (when you point at one)
│   ├── Profile page: Posts, Reels and Tagged tabs
│   └── Explore
├── Reels
│   ├── the Reels feed
│   └── opened from a reel link
├── Stories
│   ├── opened from the stories row on the home page
│   ├── opened from a profile picture
│   └── opened from a link
├── Highlights
│   ├── opened from a profile page
│   └── opened from a link
├── Dragging a post, reel or story link onto any Instagram page
├── KeepKeep's toolbar icon (the post, reel or story you are on)
├── The KeepKeep app → Downloads (anything you downloaded)
└── Import from a backup
```

### What happens

- The post is saved at once, and a small card offers to put it in one of your
  lists (keys 1–9 pick a list).
- Saved posts are marked as saved wherever they appear. Click again to remove
  it; a moment later you can still undo.
- An album is saved as one post. In a story or a highlight, the story on
  screen is saved.

### What KeepKeep keeps

- The link to the post
- The account that posted it
- What it is: photo, video, reel, album, story or highlight
- A small preview picture (Instagram's own picture links expire)
- When you saved it, and for stories, when they expire on Instagram
- Your lists it is in

### Where you find it

- **KeepKeep's toolbar icon** → Media
- **The KeepKeep app** → Media. Click a post to see it in a side panel, with
  all its photos, videos and caption.

### Good to know

- **Saving keeps the link and a preview, not the post itself.** To keep the
  photos and videos, download them ([Download in full quality](03-download.md)).
- **Stories leave Instagram after 24 hours.** A saved story keeps its preview
  in KeepKeep, but Instagram won't show the story any more. Download it to
  keep it.
- Right after an account's stories open, KeepKeep sometimes can't tell the
  first story yet, when it is a video. Go to the next story and it works.
- If Instagram doesn't answer at the moment you save, KeepKeep saves the post
  anyway and fills in its preview and owner later, by itself.
