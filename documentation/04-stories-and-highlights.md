# Download stories and highlights

Download an account's stories or a whole highlight in one click, in the same
quality as posts.

## Where it works

```
Download stories and highlights
│
├── Stories
│   ├── opened from the stories row on the home page
│   ├── opened from a profile picture
│   └── opened from a link
├── Highlights
│   ├── opened from a profile page
│   └── opened from a link
├── KeepKeep's toolbar icon (the story you are on)
└── The KeepKeep app
    ├── Media: a saved story or highlight story
    └── Downloads: download again
```

## What happens

| You download | You get |
| --- | --- |
| A story | Every story the account has up now, oldest first |
| A highlight | Every story in the highlight, in its order |
| A saved highlight story (from the app) | That one story |
| Download again (from the app) | That one story |

- Click the download button on the story, or press **D**.
- Videos follow your Video quality setting (up to 1080p), photos come in the
  largest size Instagram has.
- File names: `<account>_<date and time of the story>_story.jpg`, and
  `…_highlight.jpg` for highlights.
- Downloading itself doesn't tell the account anything. Watching a story
  does, unless [anonymous story viewing](05-anonymous-stories.md) is on.

## Good to know

- Only stories you can see on Instagram can be downloaded, and only while they
  are up (24 hours). Highlights stay as long as their owner keeps them.
- Right after an account's stories open, KeepKeep sometimes can't tell the
  first story yet, when it is a video. Go to the next story and it works.
