# KeepKeep

**Keep what you find on Instagram.**

Reels, stories, photos, videos, highlights and posts: save them to your own
lists, download them in full quality, and watch stories anonymously, right
inside Instagram.

**Never asks for your password. Your data never leaves your computer.**

No password · No tracking · No ads

## Private and secure by design

Privacy is not a feature added at the end; KeepKeep is built around it.

- **No password, no account.** KeepKeep works in the Instagram tab you are
  already signed in to.
- **Your data stays on your computer**, in your own Chrome profile. KeepKeep
  has no servers; its developer never receives anything.
- **It talks only to Instagram**, from your browser, and only to do what you
  asked.
- **No tracking, no analytics, no ads, no code from the internet.**
- **The fewest permissions it can work with**, each one explained.

More in [Private and secure by design](09-privacy-and-security.md).

## Get started

1. **Open Instagram** in Chrome and sign in as usual.
2. **Use the purple buttons** next to posts, reels, stories and profiles.
3. **Find everything in KeepKeep**: click its icon in the toolbar (pin it
   from the puzzle-piece menu), or open the KeepKeep app from there.

KeepKeep is for Chrome on a computer. It is not made by, or affiliated with,
Instagram or Meta.
