# Download in full quality (videos, photos)

Download photos and videos in the best quality Instagram has, each as its own
file, straight into `Downloads/KeepKeep`. No ZIP files, no windows, no
questions.

## Where it works

```
Download in full quality
│
├── Posts
│   ├── Home feed
│   ├── Post page (opened from a link)
│   └── Post window
│       ├── opened from a profile's grid
│       └── opened from Explore
├── Reels
│   ├── the Reels feed
│   └── opened from a reel link
├── KeepKeep's toolbar icon (the post or reel you are on)
└── The KeepKeep app
    ├── Media: one post, or many at once
    ├── the side panel of a post
    └── Downloads: download again
```

Stories and highlights: see [Anonymous story viewing and downloading](04-stories.md).

## What happens

- **The whole post, or only what's on screen.** A click downloads every photo
  and video of the post. Shift-click, or the **D** key with the pointer over
  the post, downloads only the photo or video on screen.
- **A card shows the progress** in the corner: each file with its size and
  progress, and how many are saved. It stays out of the way: fold it, or keep
  clicking KeepKeep's buttons right through it. Start another download while
  one is running and it joins the same card.
- **From the app**, downloads go through an open Instagram tab (KeepKeep
  uses your own signed-in Instagram). If none is open, the app says so and
  offers to open one.

## Photos

| Photo size | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Original** (default) | The photo as it was uploaded, up to 3072 px wide. Sharpest; files are often 1–3 MB. |
| Standard | The largest size Instagram shows, up to 1080 px wide. Smaller files. |

Instagram shows photos at up to 1080 px; KeepKeep finds the original-size
photo where Instagram keeps it. If it isn't there, you get the standard size.

## Videos

Instagram's ready-made video file stops at 720p. For videos uploaded in
higher quality, Instagram keeps the 1080p picture and the sound apart;
KeepKeep joins them into one MP4.

| Video quality | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Best** (default) | Up to 1080p, converted so it opens on every device and app. Takes a few seconds longer. |
| Original | Up to 1080p, exactly as Instagram stores it. Instant and smaller, but QuickTime, Photos, iMovie and iPhone can't open it. |
| Standard (recommended) | Up to 720p, Instagram's own single file. Instant, opens everywhere. |

- A video uploaded in 720p is the same in all three.
- **A download never fails because of the quality.** If Best can't convert a
  video (for example one longer than 10 minutes), you get Original. If the
  1080p version can't be made at all (a very large video, or an error on the
  way), you get the 720p file and the card says "720p".

## File names

`<account>_<date and time of the post>.jpg`, for example
`telma_2507271432.jpg`. Albums get `_1`, `_2`, … in their order. The date is
when the post was published, so files sort in the order they were posted.

## Good to know

- Files go to `Downloads/KeepKeep`. If you turned on Chrome's "Ask where to
  save each file", Chrome will ask for each file; KeepKeep can't change that.
- Downloading needs Instagram open in a tab, signed in.
- KeepKeep downloads only from Instagram's own servers, and only photos,
  videos and sound: an expired or wrong link is never saved as a file.
- KeepKeep doesn't download the same file twice: see
  [No duplicate downloads](07-no-duplicate-downloads.md).
