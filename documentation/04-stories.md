# Anonymous story viewing and downloading

Watch stories without appearing in the viewers list, and download an
account's stories or a whole highlight in one click.

## Watch anonymously

On by default for new installs.

### Where it works

```
Anonymous story viewing
│
├── Stories
│   ├── opened from the stories row on the home page
│   ├── opened from a profile picture
│   └── opened from a link
└── Highlights
    ├── opened from a profile page
    └── opened from a link
```

### Where you turn it on and off

```
Turn it on and off
│
├── KeepKeep's toolbar icon
│   ├── the mask button at the top
│   └── Settings → Stories
├── The KeepKeep app → Settings → Stories
└── On Instagram
    ├── the "Anonymous" badge at the top of the page
    └── "View anonymously", when Instagram asks before opening a story
```

### What happens

- **The story's owner won't see you in the viewers list.** Instagram's page
  sends a small "seen" message when you watch a story; KeepKeep answers it
  inside your browser instead of letting it go out. Nothing else is changed.
- **You can always tell it's on.** Instagram turns a soft purple, like a
  private window: the story rings get a mask badge, an "Anonymous" badge sits
  at the top of the page, and KeepKeep's toolbar icon wears a mask. Photos and
  videos are never tinted.
- **In a story**, a small badge says "@account won't see you".
- **Before you give yourself away**, KeepKeep warns you: clicking into the
  reply box says "Replying shows @account that you watched", and pointing at
  a reaction says "Reactions are visible to @account".
- **Opened from a link**, Instagram first asks "View as …? … will be able to
  see that you viewed their story". With anonymous viewing on, KeepKeep shows
  that they won't. With it off, a **View anonymously** button turns it on and
  opens the story.

### Good to know

- **Replies, reactions and likes are still visible** to the owner. KeepKeep
  only warns you.
- **Stories you watch stay unseen for you too**: their rings in your stories
  row don't turn grey.
- It works in this browser only, not in Instagram's phone app.
- On for everyone who installs KeepKeep new; if you had KeepKeep before, your
  own choice stays.

## Download stories and highlights

### Where it works

```
Download stories and highlights
│
├── Stories
│   ├── opened from the stories row on the home page
│   ├── opened from a profile picture
│   └── opened from a link
├── Highlights
│   ├── opened from a profile page
│   └── opened from a link
├── KeepKeep's toolbar icon (the story you are on)
└── The KeepKeep app
    ├── Media: a saved story or highlight story
    └── Downloads: download again
```

### What happens

| You download | You get |
| --- | --- |
| A story | Every story the account has up now, oldest first |
| A highlight | Every story in the highlight, in its order |
| A saved highlight story (from the app) | That one story |
| Download again (from the app) | That one story |

- Click the download button on the story, or press **D**.
- Videos follow your Video quality setting (up to 1080p), photos come in the
  largest size Instagram has.
- File names: `<account>_<date and time of the story>_story.jpg`, and
  `…_highlight.jpg` for highlights.
- Downloading itself doesn't tell the account anything. Watching a story
  does, unless anonymous viewing is on.

### Good to know

- Only stories you can see on Instagram can be downloaded, and only while they
  are up (24 hours). Highlights stay as long as their owner keeps them.
- Right after an account's stories open, KeepKeep sometimes can't tell the
  first story yet, when it is a video. Go to the next story and it works.
