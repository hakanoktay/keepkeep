# The KeepKeep app

Everything you saved, in a full browser tab: big grids, search, filters, and
actions on many items at once.

## Where you open it

```
Open the KeepKeep app
│
├── KeepKeep's toolbar icon → Open KeepKeep
└── by itself
    ├── after installing: the welcome page
    └── after an update with something new: What's new
```

## What's in it

```
The KeepKeep app
│
├── Media        posts, reels, videos, albums, stories, highlights
├── Profiles     accounts
├── Downloads    everything KeepKeep downloaded
├── your lists   (on Media and Profiles)
├── Settings     photo size, video quality, stories, backup
└── About        version, privacy, links, the welcome page, What's new
```

## Media and Profiles

- **Grids** with a preview, the account, when you saved it and its lists.
  New saves appear at once, while you browse Instagram in another tab.
- **Search**: by account, name or list.
- **Filters**: type (posts, reels, stories), account, list, when you saved
  it; newest or oldest first.
- **Select many** (Shift-click selects a range, ⌘/Ctrl-A everything shown),
  then: add to a list, take out of a list, download, or remove (with undo).
- **Drag** one item, or everything selected, onto a list in the sidebar.

## The side panel

Click a card and it opens on the right, without leaving the grid.

- **A post** shows itself as on Instagram: every photo and video, and the
  caption. Its lists, Download, Open on Instagram, Remove. **F** shows it
  large.
- **A profile** shows its picture, name, lists and the posts you saved from
  it. Open one of them, and a back arrow takes you back to the profile.
- **A download** shows when it was downloaded and how many files, with
  Download again and Save to Media.
- **← / →** step through the grid, **Esc** closes. ⌘/Ctrl/Shift-click on a
  card opens it on Instagram in a new tab instead.

## Good to know

- Stories don't play in the panel; it shows their saved picture. Some
  accounts don't allow their posts to be shown outside Instagram; then too
  the panel shows the saved picture and says so.
- Downloading from the app goes through an open Instagram tab.
- The app follows your computer's light or dark mode.
