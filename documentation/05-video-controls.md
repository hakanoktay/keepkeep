# Video controls

Jump to any moment of a video. KeepKeep adds a scrubber and a play / pause
button to every video on Instagram.

## Where it works

```
Video controls
│
├── Posts
│   ├── Home feed
│   ├── Post page (opened from a link)
│   └── Post window
│       ├── opened from a profile's grid
│       └── opened from Explore
└── Reels
    ├── the Reels feed
    └── opened from a reel link
```

Not in stories: they have their own timing.

## What happens

- **A thin scrubber** along the bottom of the video: click or drag to any
  point; pointing at it shows the time there.
- **A play / pause button** next to Instagram's mute button.
- **Keys**, while the pointer is over the video: **←** / **→** skip 5 seconds,
  **Space** or **K** play / pause. Elsewhere those keys do what Instagram uses
  them for.
- Instagram's own buttons, names and captions on the video stay clickable,
  and the controls step aside when something covers the video.
