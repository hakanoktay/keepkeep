# Settings

Every setting, where it is and what it starts as. Settings are the same in
the toolbar icon and in the app: change one, and the other follows.

## Where they are

```
Settings
│
├── KeepKeep's toolbar icon → Settings (⚙)
│   ├── Photo size
│   ├── Video quality
│   ├── Stories
│   └── Backup
└── The KeepKeep app
    ├── Settings
    │   ├── Photo size
    │   ├── Video quality
    │   ├── Stories
    │   └── Backup
    └── Downloads
        └── Skip downloaded before
```

## All settings

| Setting | Options | Starts as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Photo size** | Original: as uploaded, up to 3072 px wide · Standard: up to 1080 px wide | Original |
| **Video quality** | Standard: up to 720p, instant, opens everywhere (recommended) · Original: up to 1080p, as Instagram stores it · Best: up to 1080p, converted so it opens everywhere | Standard |
| **Watch stories anonymously** | on · off | On for new installs |
| **Skip downloaded before** | on · off | On |

More about each: [Download in full quality (videos, photos)](03-download.md),
[Anonymous story viewing and downloading](04-stories.md),
[No duplicate downloads](07-no-duplicate-downloads.md),
[Backup and import](08-backup.md).

## Good to know

- Downloads always go to `Downloads/KeepKeep`; there is no setting for it.
- Video controls and KeepKeep's buttons are always on.
- The app follows your computer's light or dark mode.
